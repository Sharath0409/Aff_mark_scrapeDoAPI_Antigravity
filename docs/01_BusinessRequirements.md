# RemoteProstor - Business Requirements

## Purpose

RemoteProstor exists to become one of the most trusted online resources for remote work, home office setup, productivity, and workspace improvement.

The primary objective is to help readers make better decisions by publishing practical, accurate, and experience-driven content while building a sustainable affiliate business through high-quality recommendations.

Every article published should provide genuine value to the reader before attempting to generate revenue.

---

## Vision

The long-term vision of RemoteProstor is to become a recognized authority in the remote work niche by consistently publishing high-quality content that answers real user questions and solves real workplace problems.

Rather than competing by publishing large quantities of average content, the goal is to build topical authority through depth, accuracy, consistency, and trust.

---

## Mission

RemoteProstor aims to help remote workers build healthier, more productive, and more comfortable workspaces through educational content and carefully researched buying guides.

Every published article should leave the reader with practical knowledge that can immediately improve their work environment.

---

## Target Audience

RemoteProstor is written primarily for readers in the United States.

The target audience includes:

- Remote employees
- Hybrid workers
- Freelancers
- Independent consultants
- Students studying from home
- Small business owners
- Professionals building or upgrading a home office

Content should assume readers have varying levels of technical knowledge and should remain easy to understand without oversimplifying important concepts.

---

## Business Model

RemoteProstor generates revenue primarily through affiliate marketing.

Commercial articles recommend products that genuinely solve user problems.

Informational articles educate readers, build topical authority, strengthen internal linking, and establish trust before introducing commercial recommendations where appropriate.

Revenue generation should never come at the expense of reader trust.

---

## Content Categories

The website publishes two primary types of content.

### Commercial Content

Commercial articles help readers compare products before making a purchase.

Examples include:

- Best standing desks
- Best ergonomic chairs
- Best monitor arms
- Best webcams
- Best office lighting

These articles may include affiliate links where appropriate.

---

### Informational Content

Informational articles focus on solving problems, educating readers, and improving workplace knowledge.

Examples include:

- Home office setup guides
- Ergonomic workstation guides
- Productivity techniques
- Workspace organization
- Wellness while working remotely
- Remote work best practices

These articles are designed to build authority and naturally support related commercial content through internal linking.

---

## Publishing Standards

Every published article must meet the following standards:

- Original content only.
- Written specifically for humans.
- Easy to read.
- Factually accurate.
- Practical rather than theoretical.
- Free from unnecessary filler.
- Focused on solving a specific problem.
- Optimized for search engines without keyword stuffing.
- Professionally formatted.
- Suitable for long-term evergreen value.

Publishing frequency should remain consistent while maintaining quality standards.

Quality always takes priority over quantity.

---

## Writing Principles

Every article should sound like it was written by an experienced professional rather than an AI.

Writing should be:

- Helpful
- Clear
- Honest
- Practical
- Professional
- Easy to scan
- Well structured

Avoid exaggerated claims, unnecessary repetition, clickbait, and artificial keyword placement.

The objective is to earn reader trust through useful information rather than persuasive marketing language.

---

## Content Quality Expectations

Every article should answer the questions a reader is likely to have without requiring them to visit multiple websites.

Where appropriate, content should include:

- Step-by-step guidance
- Practical examples
- Comparisons
- Checklists
- Best practices
- Common mistakes
- Frequently asked questions

Readers should finish an article feeling that their problem has been completely addressed.

---

## User Experience

Readers should be able to find information quickly.

Articles should be:

- Well organized
- Mobile friendly
- Easy to navigate
- Visually clean
- Accessible
- Fast loading

Internal links should help readers discover additional relevant content without interrupting the reading experience.

---

## Compliance

Content should follow applicable industry best practices wherever relevant.

For topics involving workstation setup, ergonomics, and workplace safety, guidance should align with recognized standards such as OSHA whenever appropriate.

Medical, legal, or financial advice should never be presented as professional consultation.

Where necessary, appropriate disclaimers should be included.

---

## Search Engine Strategy

RemoteProstor is intended to grow through organic search.

Content should focus on:

- User intent
- Topical authority
- Helpful content
- Strong internal linking
- Evergreen value
- Semantic relevance

Search engine optimization should support the reader rather than manipulate rankings.

---

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when it consistently:

- Publishes high-quality articles.
- Builds topical authority in the remote work niche.
- Increases search engine visibility.
- Earns reader trust.
- Generates sustainable affiliate revenue.
- Maintains consistent publishing quality over time.

Long-term growth is more important than short-term traffic spikes.

---

## Scope

RemoteProstor currently focuses exclusively on the following subject areas:

- Remote work
- Home office setup
- Productivity
- Ergonomics
- Workspace organization
- Wellness for remote workers
- Office equipment
- Remote collaboration

Topics outside these categories are considered out of scope unless intentionally added during a future business review.

---

## Non-Goals

RemoteProstor is not intended to become:

- A general technology news website.
- A personal blog.
- A product deals website.
- A coupon website.
- A news aggregation platform.
- A social media content platform.

The focus remains on evergreen educational content and carefully researched buying guides.

---

## Guiding Principle

Every decision made for RemoteProstor should answer one question:

> "Does this improve the reader's experience while supporting the long-term growth and credibility of the website?"

If the answer is no, the change should not be implemented.
